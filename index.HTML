Malik Baradari Cricket Platform

The Malik Baradari Cricket Platform has been established with the aim of promoting sports, unity, brotherhood, and positive activities among the youth of the Malik community. Our mission is to provide talented cricketers with opportunities to showcase their abilities and to create an environment where every player can grow and excel through the spirit of cricket. This platform is proudly supported and sponsored by Malik Rizwan Ul Haq,Founder of Malik Fallahi Jammat whose dedication, guidance, and continuous support have played a vital role in making this initiative successful. We highly appreciate his valuable contributions and commitment to the development of cricket within the community.

 Our entire management team works with sincerity, dedication, and a strong sense of teamwork to promote cricket and encourage positive engagement among the youth of the Malik Baradari. Our objective is not only to organize cricket tournaments but also to build a strong, organized, and respected sporting culture within the community. We are committed to ensuring that all decisions are made through mutual consultation, transparency, and merit, providing equal opportunities for every member and player. "Unity, Discipline, and the Spirit of Sports Define Our Identity."
